Terms and Conditions

  1. 1. Grant of Lease

    Lessor hereby leases to Lessee, and Lessee hereby leases from Lessor, the premises at 565 Whispering Pine Lane, Naples, Florida 34103 ("the Property") for the term and upon the conditions set forth in this Lease. Lessee is also a co-beneficiary of Old Naples Land Partners Trust and her occupancy rights are further governed by the Co-Beneficiary Occupancy and Equity Agreement of even date herewith.

  2. 2. Rent

    Lessee shall pay Lessor rent in the amount of $11,000.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each calendar month. Rent represents interest only on Carl Fernstrom's $2,700,000.00 beneficial interest contribution to Old Naples Land Partners Trust at a fixed rate of 4.9% per annum. No portion of the monthly rent constitutes a reduction of principal.

    Rent shall be paid by check, wire transfer, or such other method as the parties mutually agree in writing. Lessee shall not be required to pay any security deposit under this Lease.

  3. 3. Lease Term

    This Lease shall commence on __________________, 2026 and shall continue for a period of one (1) year, ending on __________________, 2027, unless sooner terminated pursuant to Section 4 of this Lease.

  4. 4. Early Termination Upon Sale of Current Homestead

    This Lease shall automatically terminate upon the closing of the sale of Lessee's current homestead property located at 5160 Crayton Road, Naples, Florida. Upon such sale:

    • This Lease shall terminate by operation of this Agreement without further notice or action by either party;
    • Lessee's rent obligation under this Lease shall cease as of the date of that closing;
    • The exit plan set forth in the Co-Beneficiary Occupancy and Equity Agreement shall be initiated, pursuant to which Lessee shall obtain a mortgage, the Trust shall be dissolved, and title to 565 Whispering Pine Lane shall transfer into Lessee's name individually.
    Note: Upon termination of this Lease and transfer of title to Heather Fernstrom Border individually, 565 Whispering Pine Lane shall cease to be an investment property and shall become Lessee's Florida Homestead primary residence.

  5. 5. Lessee's Exclusive Occupancy and Access Rights

    Lessee shall have full, sole, and exclusive right of occupancy of the Property during the Lease term. Lessee shall have the right to:

    • Occupy the Property as her primary residence;
    • Change locks, security codes, gate codes, and alarm credentials at any time and without notice to Lessor or any co-beneficiary of the Trust;
    • Invite or exclude any person from the Property at her sole discretion;
    • Quiet enjoyment of the Property free from interference by any party.

  6. 6. Use of Property

    The Property shall be used solely for lawful residential purposes. Lessee shall comply with all applicable laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the use and occupancy of the Property.

  7. 7. Maintenance and Repairs

    Lessee shall maintain the Property in good condition and shall be responsible for routine maintenance and minor repairs. Lessee shall promptly notify the Trustee of any material damage to the Property. Lessee shall have the right to make reasonable improvements and alterations to the Property with prior written consent of the Trustee, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

  8. 8. Utilities and Services

    Lessee shall be responsible for establishing and paying all utility accounts in her own name, including electricity, water, gas, internet, cable, and any other services for the Property. No party shall interfere with, interrupt, or attempt to terminate any utility service to the Property.

  9. 9. Trustee and Co-Beneficiary Access

    Neither the Trustee nor Carl Fernstrom (as co-beneficiary of Old Naples Land Partners Trust) shall have any right of entry, access, or use of the Property at any time during the Lease term without the prior written consent of Lessee. All rights of entry are expressly waived except as required by a final, non-appealable court order.

  10. 10. Governing Law

    This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any dispute arising hereunder shall be brought exclusively in the courts of Collier County, Florida.

  11. 11. Entire Agreement

    This Lease, together with the Old Naples Land Partners Trust Co-Beneficiary Occupancy and Equity Agreement of even date herewith, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to Lessee's occupancy of the Property. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

  12. 12. Acknowledgment

    By signing below, both parties acknowledge they have read, understood, and voluntarily agree to the terms of this Lease. Both parties are strongly encouraged to seek independent legal counsel prior to signing.
