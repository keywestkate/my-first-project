Terms and Conditions

  1. 1. Florida Land Trust Structure

    Legal title to the Property shall be held by a Florida Land Trust ("the Trust"), established at or before closing pursuant to Florida Statute § 689.071. A trustee — to be designated by mutual written agreement of both parties prior to closing — shall hold legal title solely as trustee and shall have no beneficial interest in the Property.

    Heather Fernstrom and Carl Fernstrom shall be the sole co-beneficiaries of the Trust with beneficial interests as set forth in the Equity Contributions table above. Carl Fernstrom's beneficial interest is structured in part to facilitate a potential IRC § 1031 like-kind exchange as described in Section 9.

  2. 2. Heather Fernstrom's Equity Contribution

    Heather Fernstrom's contribution of $1,800,000.00 is an equity investment representing a 45% beneficial interest in the Trust. This contribution:

    • Is not a loan and carries no obligation of repayment to Carl Fernstrom;
    • Establishes Heather Fernstrom as a co-beneficiary of the Trust with a 45% beneficial interest;
    • Shall be credited in full toward Heather Fernstrom's buyout of Carl Fernstrom's interest (see Section 8);
    • Cannot be diluted, reduced, or challenged by Carl Fernstrom on any basis whatsoever.

  3. 3. Heather Fernstrom's Full and Exclusive Occupancy Rights

    Heather Fernstrom shall have full, sole, and exclusive legal right of occupancy of the Property. As the occupying co-beneficiary, and notwithstanding Carl Fernstrom's majority beneficial interest, Heather Fernstrom's occupancy rights include, without limitation:

    • The right to occupy and use the Property as her primary residence without interference from any party;
    • The right to control all access to the Property, including the right to change locks, security codes, gate codes, and alarm credentials at any time and without notice to Carl Fernstrom;
    • The right to make reasonable improvements, alterations, and decorative changes to the Property;
    • The right to invite or exclude any person from the Property at her sole discretion, expressly including Carl Fernstrom;
    • The right to quiet enjoyment of the Property free from interference by Carl Fernstrom or any party acting on his behalf.

    Carl Fernstrom's status as a co-beneficiary of the Trust does not grant him any right of entry, access, use, or occupancy of the Property at any time, for any reason.

  5. 5. Carl Fernstrom's Prohibited Actions as Co-Beneficiary

    Notwithstanding Carl Fernstrom's status as a co-beneficiary, Carl Fernstrom expressly agrees that he shall not, under any circumstances — including any claimed default on interest payments — take any of the following actions:

    • (a) Enter or attempt to enter the Property without prior written consent from Heather Fernstrom;
    • (b) Possess, use, or retain any key, access code, garage opener, gate code, or any other means of entry to the Property;
    • (c) Change, alter, rekey, or disable any lock, security system, alarm, gate, or access point at the Property;
    • (d) Lock out, exclude, or remove Heather Fernstrom or her personal property from the Property by any means;
    • (e) Interfere with, interrupt, or attempt to terminate any utility service to the Property, including electricity, water, gas, internet, or cable;
    • (f) Direct, instruct, or engage any third party — including the trustee — to take any action that interferes with Heather Fernstrom's occupancy without her prior written consent;
    • (g) Attempt to sell, transfer, encumber, or otherwise dispose of his beneficial interest in the Trust without Heather Fernstrom's prior written consent;
    • (h) Take any action — legal, physical, or through the trustee — that would prevent, delay, or obstruct Heather Fernstrom's access to or enjoyment of the Property.

    All remedies available to Carl Fernstrom upon a claimed default of interest payments are limited exclusively to formal legal proceedings in Collier County Circuit Court following the cure periods in Section 6. Carl Fernstrom expressly waives all self-help remedies and may not take possession of or control over the Property without a final, non-appealable court order.

    In the event Heather Fernstrom fails to make a scheduled annual interest payment, the following cure periods apply before Carl Fernstrom may pursue any legal remedy:

  7. 7. Non-Disturbance and Anti-Coercion

    Carl Fernstrom covenants and agrees that regardless of any personal, financial, or family dispute between the parties; any claimed default on interest payments; any change in his personal financial circumstances; or any demands made by his line of credit lender — Carl Fernstrom shall not disturb, threaten, or interfere with Heather Fernstrom's possession and enjoyment of the Property.

    Carl Fernstrom shall not use his co-beneficiary status, his equity contribution, or any financial relationship with Heather Fernstrom as a threat or instrument of coercion, including but not limited to:

    • Threatening to "take the house," transfer his beneficial interest, or direct the trustee to act against Heather Fernstrom's interests in any personal dispute;
    • Threatening legal action outside of the formal written notice process in Section 6;
    • Using access or the threat of access to the Property to influence Heather Fernstrom's personal decisions.

    Any such conduct shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement and shall entitle Heather Fernstrom to seek immediate injunctive relief without bond in Collier County Circuit Court.

  9. 9. Carl Fernstrom's 1031 Exchange — Acknowledgment and Limitations

    The parties acknowledge that Carl Fernstrom intends to utilize his beneficial interest in this transaction as a replacement property in an IRC § 1031 like-kind exchange in order to defer capital gains taxes from the sale of a prior investment property. Both parties acknowledge:

    • Carl Fernstrom's 1031 exchange objective does not create any additional rights over the Property or over Heather Fernstrom's occupancy, access, or enjoyment of the Property;
    • Heather Fernstrom makes no representation or warranty as to the tax qualification of Carl Fernstrom's 1031 exchange — Carl Fernstrom is solely responsible for confirming qualification with his tax advisor and attorney prior to closing;
    • In the event Carl Fernstrom's 1031 exchange is disqualified for any reason, that disqualification shall have no effect on Heather Fernstrom's rights under this Agreement, her occupancy, or the buyout terms in Section 8;
    • Carl Fernstrom shall not use any 1031 exchange requirement, IRS demand, or tax matter as justification for any action prohibited under Section 5 of this Agreement.

  10. 10. Protection from Carl Fernstrom's Personal Creditors

Carl Fernstrom's personal debt obligations shall not be used to place any lien on the Property. Carl Fernstrom agrees to indemnify and hold Heather Fernstrom harmless from any claim, cost, or damage arising from Carl Fernstrom's personal financial obligations.
