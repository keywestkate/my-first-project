Terms and Conditions

  1. 1. Trust Structure — Citadel Equities, a Florida Land Trust

    Legal title to the Property shall be held by Citadel Equities, a Florida Land Trust, a Florida Land Trust ("the Trust"), established at or before closing pursuant to Florida Statute § 689.071. Nace Cohen, as Trustee, shall hold legal title to the Property solely in a trustee capacity and shall have no beneficial interest in the Property.

    Heather Fernstrom Border and Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd. shall be the sole co-beneficiaries of the Trust with beneficial interests as set forth in the Equity Contributions table above.

  2. 2. Trustee Authority and Limitations

    Both parties acknowledge that Nace Cohen serves as Trustee of Citadel Equities, a Florida Land Trust. Both parties expressly agree that neither party may direct, instruct, or influence the Trustee to take any action affecting the Property without the prior written consent of the other party. These restrictions apply equally to both Heather Fernstrom Border and Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd. Specifically:

    • The Trustee shall not act on any instruction from either party alone — any trustee direction requires written consent of both parties;
    • Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd.'s 55% interest is a financial interest only — it grants no unilateral authority over the Property or Heather Fernstrom Border's occupancy;
    • Heather Fernstrom Border's 45% interest is an occupancy and equity interest — it grants no unilateral authority to direct the Trustee in matters affecting Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd.'s financial interest;
    • The Trustee shall receive a copy of this Agreement at or before closing and shall be bound by its terms;
    • Any unilateral attempt by either party to direct the Trustee adverse to the other party's interests constitutes a material breach entitling the non-breaching party to immediate injunctive relief without bond.

  3. 3. Heather Fernstrom Border's Equity Contribution

    Heather Fernstrom Border's contribution of $2,200,000.00 is an equity investment representing a 45% beneficial interest in the Trust. It is not a loan, carries no repayment obligation, shall be credited in full toward her buyout of Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd.'s interest (see Section 7), and cannot be diluted or challenged by Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd. on any basis.

  4. 4. Heather Fernstrom Border's Full and Exclusive Occupancy Rights

    Heather Fernstrom Border shall have full, sole, and exclusive legal right of occupancy of the Property. As the occupying co-beneficiary, and notwithstanding Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd.'s majority beneficial interest, Heather Fernstrom Border's occupancy rights include, without limitation:

    • The right to occupy and use the Property as her primary residence without interference from any party;
    • The right to control all access to the Property, including the right to change locks, security codes, gate codes, and alarm credentials at any time and without notice to Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd..

  5. 5. Lease of Property — Interest Only

    Heather Fernstrom Border shall lease 565 Whispering Pine Lane from Citadel Equities, a Florida Land Trust at a monthly lease payment of $9,450.00, paid to Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd.. No principal reduction shall occur during the lease term.

    Upon the sale of the Current Homestead at 5160 Crayton Road, Naples, Florida, this Lease shall automatically terminate and the Trust shall be dissolved per Section 7 below.

  6. 6. Lessee's Responsibility for Taxes, Utilities, and Maintenance

    During the lease term, Heather Fernstrom Border shall be solely responsible for the following:

    • Property Taxes: All real property taxes and assessments levied against 565 Whispering Pine Lane during the lease term;
    • Utilities: All utility services including electricity, water, sewer, gas, internet, cable, and any other services to the Property;
    • Maintenance: All routine maintenance and upkeep of the Property, including landscaping, pest control, pool service, and minor repairs necessary to keep the Property in good condition.

  7. 7. Exit Plan — Transfer and Florida Homestead Designation

    ★ Heather Fernstrom Border's Path to Full Ownership and Homestead

    Current Status of Properties:

    • 5160 Crayton Road, Naples, Florida — Heather Fernstrom Border's current Florida Homestead primary residence ("Current Homestead").
    • 565 Whispering Pine Lane, Naples, Florida 34103 — Held in Citadel Equities, a Florida Land Trust as an investment property during the interim period until the Current Homestead sells.

    The following steps shall be triggered automatically upon the sale of the Current Homestead:

    • Step 1 — Heather Obtains a Mortgage: Heather Fernstrom Border shall obtain a conventional mortgage in her own name sufficient to buy out Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd.'s beneficial interest in Citadel Equities, a Florida Land Trust. She shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain financing within 90 days of the Current Homestead sale closing.
    • Step 2 — Automatic Trust Dissolution and Title Transfer: Upon completion of the buyout, Citadel Equities, a Florida Land Trust shall be automatically dissolved. Legal title to 565 Whispering Pine Lane shall transfer automatically to Heather Fernstrom Border individually, free and clear of Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd.'s interest. The Property shall automatically cease to be an investment property at this point.
    • Step 3 — Florida Homestead Declaration: Heather Fernstrom Border shall file a Declaration of Homestead with Collier County pursuant to Florida Statute § 196.031, designating 565 Whispering Pine Lane as her Florida Homestead primary residence.

    Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd. agrees to cooperate fully and promptly with all steps above and shall execute all necessary documents within 30 days of written request by Heather Fernstrom Border. Olde Naples Land Partners Ltd. shall not withhold, delay, or impose conditions on his cooperation. Time is of the essence.

  8. 8. Entire Agreement / Severability

    This Agreement, together with the Citadel Equities Trust Agreement and related closing documents, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the Property. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.
