Terms and Conditions

  1. 1. Trust Structure — Old Naples Land Partners

    Legal title to the Property shall be held by Old Naples Land Partners, a Florida Land Trust ("the Trust"), established at or before closing pursuant to Florida Statute § 689.071. A trustee — to be designated by mutual written agreement of both parties prior to closing — shall hold legal title solely as trustee and shall have no beneficial interest in the Property.

    Heather Fernstrom Border and Carl Fernstrom shall be the sole co-beneficiaries of the Trust with beneficial interests as set forth in the Equity Contributions table above. Carl Fernstrom's beneficial interest is structured in part to facilitate a potential IRC § 1031 like-kind exchange as described in Section 9.

  2. 2. Trustee Authority and Limitations

    The parties acknowledge that Carl Fernstrom holds a 55% majority beneficial interest in the Trust. Both parties expressly agree that Carl Fernstrom's majority interest does not grant him the authority to direct, instruct, or influence the trustee to take any action affecting the Property without the prior written consent of Heather Fernstrom Border. Specifically:

    • The trustee shall not act on any instruction from Carl Fernstrom alone regarding the Property, its occupancy, its management, or any conveyance of title without Heather Fernstrom Border's prior written consent;
    • Any direction to the trustee regarding the Property must be agreed upon in writing by both Heather Fernstrom Border and Carl Fernstrom before the trustee may act;
    • Carl Fernstrom's majority beneficial interest is a financial interest only — it carries no unilateral right to control, direct, or influence trustee decisions related to the Property or Heather Fernstrom Border's occupancy;
    • The trustee shall be provided a copy of this Agreement at or before closing and shall be bound by its terms as a condition of serving as trustee;
    • Any attempt by Carl Fernstrom to direct the trustee unilaterally in a manner adverse to Heather Fernstrom Border's interests shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement and shall entitle Heather Fernstrom Border to seek immediate injunctive relief without bond.

  3. 3. Heather Fernstrom Border's Equity Contribution

    Heather Fernstrom Border's contribution of $2,205,000.00 is an equity investment representing a 45% beneficial interest in the Trust. This contribution:

    • Is not a loan and carries no obligation of repayment to Carl Fernstrom;
    • Establishes Heather Fernstrom Border as a co-beneficiary of the Trust with a 45% beneficial interest;
    • Shall be credited in full toward Heather Fernstrom Border's buyout of Carl Fernstrom's interest (see Section 8);
    • Cannot be diluted, reduced, or challenged by Carl Fernstrom on any basis whatsoever.

  4. 4. Heather Fernstrom Border's Full and Exclusive Occupancy Rights

    Heather Fernstrom Border shall have full, sole, and exclusive legal right of occupancy of the Property. As the occupying co-beneficiary, and notwithstanding Carl Fernstrom's majority beneficial interest, Heather Fernstrom Border's occupancy rights include, without limitation:

    • The right to occupy and use the Property as her primary residence without interference from any party;
    • The right to control all access to the Property, including the right to change locks, security codes, gate codes, and alarm credentials at any time and without notice to Carl Fernstrom;
    • The right to make reasonable improvements, alterations, and decorative changes to the Property;
    • The right to invite or exclude any person from the Property at her sole discretion, expressly including Carl Fernstrom;
    • The right to quiet enjoyment of the Property free from interference by Carl Fernstrom or any party acting on his behalf.

    Carl Fernstrom's status as a co-beneficiary of the Trust does not grant him any right of entry, access, use, or occupancy of the Property at any time, for any reason.

  5. 5. Interest Only

    Heather Fernstrom Border agrees to pay Carl Fernstrom interest only on his contributed amount of $2,695,000.00 at a fixed rate of 4.9% per annum. No principal shall be paid down during the interim period. Carl Fernstrom's balance remains at $2,695,000.00 until buyout under the exit plan in Section 8.

  6. 6. Carl Fernstrom's Prohibited Actions as Co-Beneficiary

    Notwithstanding Carl Fernstrom's status as a co-beneficiary, Carl Fernstrom expressly agrees that he shall not, under any circumstances — including any claimed default on interest payments — take any of the following actions:

    • (a) Enter or attempt to enter the Property without prior written consent from Heather Fernstrom Border;
    • (b) Possess, use, or retain any key, access code, garage opener, gate code, or any other means of entry to the Property;
    • (c) Change, alter, rekey, or disable any lock, security system, alarm, gate, or access point at the Property;
    • (d) Lock out, exclude, or remove Heather Fernstrom Border or her personal property from the Property by any means;
    • (e) Interfere with, interrupt, or attempt to terminate any utility service to the Property, including electricity, water, gas, internet, or cable;
    • (f) Direct, instruct, or engage any third party — including the trustee — to take any action that interferes with Heather Fernstrom Border's occupancy without her prior written consent;
    • (g) Attempt to sell, transfer, encumber, or otherwise dispose of his beneficial interest in the Trust without Heather Fernstrom Border's prior written consent;
    • (h) Take any action — legal, physical, or through the trustee — that would prevent, delay, or obstruct Heather Fernstrom Border's access to or enjoyment of the Property.

    All remedies available to Carl Fernstrom are limited exclusively to formal legal proceedings in Collier County Circuit Court. Carl Fernstrom expressly waives all self-help remedies and may not take possession of or control over the Property without a final, non-appealable court order.

  7. 7. Non-Disturbance and Anti-Coercion

    Carl Fernstrom covenants and agrees that regardless of any personal, financial, or family dispute between the parties; any claimed default on interest payments; any change in his personal financial circumstances; or any demands made by his line of credit lender — Carl Fernstrom shall not disturb, threaten, or interfere with Heather Fernstrom Border's possession and enjoyment of the Property.

    Carl Fernstrom shall not use his co-beneficiary status, his equity contribution, or any financial relationship with Heather Fernstrom Border as a threat or instrument of coercion, including but not limited to:

    • Threatening to "take the house," transfer his beneficial interest, or direct the trustee to act against Heather Fernstrom Border's interests in any personal dispute;
    • Threatening legal action or legal proceedings against Heather Fernstrom Border in connection with the Property;
    • Using access or the threat of access to the Property to influence Heather Fernstrom Border's personal decisions.

    Any such conduct shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement and shall entitle Heather Fernstrom to seek immediate injunctive relief without bond in Collier County Circuit Court.

  8. 8. Exit Plan — Transfer, Buyout, and Florida Homestead Designation

    ★ Heather Fernstrom Border's Path to Full Ownership and Homestead

    Current Status of Properties:

    • 5160 Crayton Road, Naples, Florida — Heather Fernstrom Border's current Florida Homestead primary residence ("Current Homestead").
    • 565 Whispering Pine Lane, Naples, Florida 34103 — Held in Old Naples Land Partners as an investment property during the interim period until the Current Homestead sells.

    The parties expressly agree to the following exit and transfer process, which shall be triggered upon the sale of the Current Homestead:

    • Step 1 — Sale of 5160 Crayton Road (Current Homestead): Upon the closing of the sale of Heather Fernstrom Border's current homestead at 5160 Crayton Road, Naples, Florida, the exit process described in Steps 2 through 5 below shall be initiated. Heather Fernstrom Border shall provide Carl Fernstrom written notice of the sale closing within 5 business days.
    • Step 2 — Heather Obtains a Mortgage: Following the sale of the Current Homestead, Heather Fernstrom Border shall obtain a conventional mortgage in her own name in an amount sufficient to purchase Carl Fernstrom's beneficial interest in Old Naples Land Partners (approximately $2,695,000.00 plus any accrued and unpaid interest, less any prior principal credits). Heather Fernstrom Border shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain mortgage financing within 90 days of the Current Homestead sale closing.
    • Step 3 — Buyout of Carl Fernstrom's Beneficial Interest: Using mortgage proceeds, Heather Fernstrom Border shall purchase Carl Fernstrom's full beneficial interest in Old Naples Land Partners. The buyout price shall equal Carl's original equity contribution ($2,695,000.00) plus any accrued and unpaid annual interest at 4.9%, minus any amounts previously credited toward principal reduction. [Attorney to finalize exact formula prior to execution.]
    • Step 4 — Trust Dissolution and Title Transfer: Upon completion of the buyout, Old Naples Land Partners shall be dissolved. Legal title to 565 Whispering Pine Lane shall be transferred to Heather Fernstrom Border individually, free and clear of Carl Fernstrom's beneficial interest.
    • Step 5 — Florida Homestead Declaration: Following the transfer of title into Heather Fernstrom Border's name, she shall file a Declaration of Homestead with Collier County pursuant to Florida Statute § 196.031, designating 565 Whispering Pine Lane as her Florida Homestead primary residence pursuant to Article X, Section 4 of the Florida Constitution. At this point, 565 Whispering Pine Lane shall cease to be an investment property and shall become Heather Fernstrom Border's primary residence.

    Carl Fernstrom agrees to cooperate fully and promptly with all steps above and shall execute any and all documents necessary to effectuate the buyout, trust dissolution, and title transfer within 30 days of written request by Heather Fernstrom Border. Carl Fernstrom shall not withhold, delay, or impose conditions on his cooperation for any reason. Time is of the essence with respect to all steps in this Section.

  9. 9. Carl Fernstrom's 1031 Exchange — Acknowledgment and Limitations

    The parties acknowledge that Carl Fernstrom intends to utilize his beneficial interest in this transaction as a replacement property in an IRC § 1031 like-kind exchange in order to defer capital gains taxes from the sale of a prior investment property.

    The parties note that 565 Whispering Pine Lane is classified as an investment property during the interim period while Heather Fernstrom Border's current homestead at 5160 Crayton Road, Naples, Florida remains her primary residence. This investment property classification supports the qualification of Carl Fernstrom's beneficial interest in Old Naples Land Partners as a valid 1031 replacement property. Both parties acknowledge:

    • The Property is held as an investment property within Old Naples Land Partners during the interim period — it is not Heather Fernstrom Border's primary residence until Step 5 of the exit plan in Section 8 is completed;
    • Carl Fernstrom's 1031 exchange objective does not create any additional rights over the Property or over Heather Fernstrom Border's access to or use of the Property;
    • Heather Fernstrom Border makes no representation or warranty as to the tax qualification of Carl Fernstrom's 1031 exchange — Carl Fernstrom is solely responsible for confirming qualification with his tax advisor and attorney prior to closing;
    • In the event Carl Fernstrom's 1031 exchange is disqualified for any reason, that disqualification shall have no effect on Heather Fernstrom Border's rights under this Agreement or the buyout terms in Section 8;
    • Carl Fernstrom shall not use any 1031 exchange requirement, IRS demand, or tax matter as justification for any action prohibited under Section 6 of this Agreement.
    Note for Attorney: The investment property classification of 565 Whispering Pine Lane during the interim period (while 5160 Crayton Road remains Heather's homestead) strengthens Carl's 1031 qualification position. Attorney to confirm this structure satisfies IRC § 1031 holding requirements and advise on minimum hold period before the property converts to Heather's primary residence under the exit plan.

  10. 10. Protection from Carl Fernstrom's Personal Creditors

    Carl Fernstrom represents and warrants that his personal line of credit used to fund his equity contribution does not and shall not create any lien, claim, or encumbrance against the Property itself. Carl Fernstrom's personal debt obligations shall not be used to place any lien on the Property. Carl Fernstrom agrees to indemnify and hold Heather Fernstrom Border harmless from any claim, cost, or damage arising from Carl Fernstrom's personal financial obligations.

  11. 11. Remedies for Breach

    If Carl Fernstrom breaches any provision of this Agreement, Heather Fernstrom Border shall be entitled to:

    • (a) Immediate injunctive relief in Collier County Circuit Court without the necessity of posting bond;
    • (b) Actual damages, including all costs resulting from the breach (locksmith, temporary housing, legal fees, etc.);
    • (c) Attorney's fees and court costs pursuant to Florida Statute § 57.105;
    • (d) Specific performance compelling Carl Fernstrom to execute transfer and dissolution documents under Section 8;
    • (e) Any other remedy available at law or in equity.

    Carl Fernstrom acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to Heather Fernstrom Border for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate.

  12. 12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

    This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any dispute arising hereunder shall be brought exclusively in the courts of Collier County, Florida.

  13. 13. Entire Agreement / Severability

    This Agreement, together with the Old Naples Land Partners Trust Agreement, Promissory Note, and related closing documents, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding their respective rights and obligations with respect to the Property. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
