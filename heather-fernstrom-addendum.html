Terms and Conditions

  1. 1. Trust Structure — Old Naples Land Partners Trust

    Legal title to the Property shall be held by Old Naples Land Partners Trust, a Florida Land Trust ("the Trust"), established at or before closing pursuant to Florida Statute § 689.071. A trustee — to be designated by mutual written agreement of both parties prior to closing — shall hold legal title solely as trustee and shall have no beneficial interest in the Property.

    Heather Fernstrom Border and Carl Fernstrom shall be the sole co-beneficiaries of the Trust with beneficial interests as set forth in the Equity Contributions table above.

  2. 2. Trustee Authority and Limitations

    The parties acknowledge that Carl Fernstrom holds a 55% majority beneficial interest in the Trust. Both parties expressly agree that Carl Fernstrom's majority interest does not grant him the authority to direct, instruct, or influence the trustee to take any action affecting the Property without the prior written consent of Heather Fernstrom Border. Specifically:

    • The trustee shall not act on any instruction from Carl Fernstrom alone — any trustee direction requires written consent of both parties;
    • Carl Fernstrom's 55% interest is a financial interest only — it grants no unilateral authority over the Property or Heather Fernstrom Border's occupancy;
    • The trustee shall receive a copy of this Agreement at or before closing and shall be bound by its terms;
    • Any unilateral attempt by Carl Fernstrom to direct the trustee adverse to Heather Fernstrom Border's interests constitutes a material breach entitling Heather Fernstrom Border to immediate injunctive relief without bond.

  3. 3. Heather Fernstrom Border's Equity Contribution

    Heather Fernstrom Border's contribution of $2,200,000.00 is an equity investment representing a 45% beneficial interest in the Trust. It is not a loan, carries no repayment obligation, shall be credited in full toward her buyout of Carl Fernstrom's interest (see Section 6), and cannot be diluted or challenged by Carl Fernstrom on any basis.

  4. 4. Heather Fernstrom Border's Full and Exclusive Occupancy Rights

    Heather Fernstrom Border shall have full, sole, and exclusive legal right of occupancy of the Property. As the occupying co-beneficiary, and notwithstanding Carl Fernstrom's majority beneficial interest, Heather Fernstrom Border's occupancy rights include, without limitation:

    • The right to occupy and use the Property as her primary residence without interference from any party;
    • The right to control all access to the Property, including the right to change locks, security codes, gate codes, and alarm credentials at any time and without notice to Carl Fernstrom.

    Heather Fernstrom Border further retains the right to make improvements, to invite or exclude any person (expressly including Carl Fernstrom) at her sole discretion, and to quiet enjoyment free from interference by Carl Fernstrom or any party acting on his behalf.

    Carl Fernstrom's status as a co-beneficiary of the Trust does not grant him any right of entry, access, use, or occupancy of the Property at any time, for any reason.

  5. 5. Interest Only

    Heather Fernstrom Border shall pay the Trust $11,000.00 per month, interest only. Upon the sale of the Current Homestead at 5516 Place South, Naples, Florida, this interest obligation shall automatically terminate and the Trust shall be dissolved per Section 6 below.

  6. 6. Exit Plan — Transfer and Florida Homestead Designation

    ★ Heather Fernstrom Border's Path to Full Ownership and Homestead

    Current Status of Properties:

    • 5516 Place South, Naples, Florida — Heather Fernstrom Border's current Florida Homestead primary residence ("Current Homestead").
    • 565 Whispering Pine Lane, Naples, Florida 34103 — Held in Old Naples Land Partners Trust as an investment property during the interim period until the Current Homestead sells.

    The following steps shall be triggered automatically upon the sale of the Current Homestead:

    • Step 1 — Sale of 5516 Place South, Naples, Florida (Current Homestead): Upon closing, Heather Fernstrom Border shall provide Carl Fernstrom written notice within 5 business days.
    • Step 2 — Heather Obtains a Mortgage: Heather Fernstrom Border shall obtain a conventional mortgage in her own name sufficient to buy out Carl Fernstrom's beneficial interest in Old Naples Land Partners Trust. She shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain financing within 90 days of the Current Homestead sale closing.
    • Step 3 — Automatic Trust Dissolution and Title Transfer: Upon completion of the buyout, Old Naples Land Partners Trust shall be automatically dissolved. Legal title to 565 Whispering Pine Lane shall transfer automatically to Heather Fernstrom Border individually, free and clear of Carl Fernstrom's interest. The Property shall automatically cease to be an investment property at this point.
    • Step 4 — Florida Homestead Declaration: Heather Fernstrom Border shall file a Declaration of Homestead with Collier County pursuant to Florida Statute § 196.031, designating 565 Whispering Pine Lane as her Florida Homestead primary residence.

    Carl Fernstrom agrees to cooperate fully and promptly with all steps above and shall execute all necessary documents within 30 days of written request by Heather Fernstrom Border. Carl Fernstrom shall not withhold, delay, or impose conditions on his cooperation. Time is of the essence.

  7. 8. Remedies for Breach

    If Carl Fernstrom breaches any provision of this Agreement, Heather Fernstrom Border shall be entitled to:

    • (a) Immediate injunctive relief in Collier County Circuit Court without the necessity of posting bond;
    • (b) Actual damages, including all costs resulting from the breach (locksmith, temporary housing, legal fees, etc.);
    • (c) Attorney's fees and court costs pursuant to Florida Statute § 57.105;
    • (d) Specific performance compelling Carl Fernstrom to execute transfer and dissolution documents under Section 6;
    • (e) Any other remedy available at law or in equity.

    Carl Fernstrom acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to Heather Fernstrom Border for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate.

  8. 8. Entire Agreement / Severability

    This Agreement, together with the Old Naples Land Partners Trust Agreement and related closing documents, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the Property. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.
