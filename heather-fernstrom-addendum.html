Terms and Conditions

  1. 1. Lender Status Only

    Carl Fernstrom's sole relationship to the Property is that of a private lender. Carl Fernstrom holds no ownership interest, possessory interest, or right of occupancy in the Property. The fact that Carl Fernstrom has contributed funds toward the purchase of the Property does not grant him any right to access, use, occupy, or control the Property in any manner whatsoever.

  2. 2. Heather's Exclusive Right of Occupancy

    Heather Fernstrom shall have sole, exclusive, and quiet enjoyment of the Property. This includes, without limitation:

    • The right to occupy the Property as her primary residence and Florida Homestead;
    • The right to control all access to the Property;
    • The right to change locks, security codes, and access credentials at any time without notice to Carl Fernstrom;
    • The right to make reasonable improvements or alterations to the Property;
    • The right to invite or exclude any person from the Property at her sole discretion, including Carl Fernstrom.

  3. 3. Florida Homestead Protection

    ★ Florida Homestead Designation

    The parties acknowledge and agree that the Property located at 565 Whispering Pine Lane, Naples, Florida 34103 is designated as Heather Fernstrom's primary residence and Florida Homestead pursuant to Article X, Section 4 of the Florida Constitution and applicable Florida Statutes.

    As a Florida Homestead property, the Property is afforded the following additional protections which Carl Fernstrom expressly acknowledges and agrees not to circumvent:

    • The Property cannot be seized or forced to be sold to satisfy the debts of Carl Fernstrom, including any debts Carl Fernstrom may have related to his personal line of credit used to fund the Loan;
    • Carl Fernstrom's lien interest, if any, does not supersede Heather Fernstrom's homestead rights under Florida law;
    • No foreclosure or lien enforcement action may be brought except in strict compliance with Florida law and the cure periods set forth herein;
    • Carl Fernstrom waives any claim that his financial contribution to the purchase price creates a resulting trust, constructive trust, or equitable interest in the Property superior to Heather Fernstrom's homestead rights.

  4. 4. Carl Fernstrom's Prohibited Actions

    Carl Fernstrom expressly agrees that he shall not, under any circumstances:

    • (a) Enter or attempt to enter the Property without prior written consent from Heather Fernstrom;
    • (b) Possess, use, or retain any key, access code, garage opener, gate code, or any other means of entry to the Property;
    • (c) Change, alter, rekey, or disable any lock, security system, alarm, gate, or access point at the Property;
    • (d) Interfere with, interrupt, or attempt to terminate any utility service to the Property including but not limited to electricity, water, gas, internet, or cable;
    • (e) Remove, damage, or dispose of any personal property belonging to Heather Fernstrom at the Property;
    • (f) Contact, instruct, or engage any third party to access or interfere with the Property on his behalf;
    • (g) Record any lien, lis pendens, encumbrance, or cloud on title to the Property except as expressly permitted under the Promissory Note and Deed of Trust recorded at closing;
    • (h) Take any action — legal, physical, or otherwise — that would prevent, delay, or obstruct Heather Fernstrom's access to or enjoyment of the Property.

  5. 5. No Self-Help Remedies

    Carl Fernstrom expressly waives any right to self-help remedies in connection with the Property. Under no circumstances, including a claimed default by Heather Fernstrom, may Carl Fernstrom:

    • Physically lock out or exclude Heather Fernstrom from the Property;
    • Remove Heather Fernstrom's belongings from the Property;
    • Take possession of the Property without a final, non-appealable court order from a court of competent jurisdiction in Collier County, Florida.

    All remedies available to Carl Fernstrom upon default are limited exclusively to legal proceedings as set forth in Section 6 below.

  6. 6. Default and Cure Periods

    In the event Heather Fernstrom fails to make a scheduled loan payment, the following cure periods shall apply before Carl Fernstrom may pursue any legal remedy:

    Event Requirement Timeframe
    Written Notice of Default Must be delivered via certified mail AND email to Heather Fernstrom Day 1 — starts the clock
    First Cure Period Heather may cure by making the missed payment in full 30 days from receipt of written notice
    Extended Cure Period If Heather provides written notice of hardship within first 30 days 60 days from original written notice
    Final Cure Period No legal action may be initiated before this period expires without written mutual agreement 90 days from original written notice
    During any active cure period, all provisions of this Addendum remain in full force. Carl Fernstrom may not take any action described in Sections 4 or 5 regardless of payment status.

    Carl Fernstrom covenants and agrees that regardless of:

    • Any dispute between Carl Fernstrom and Heather Fernstrom of a personal, financial, or family nature;
    • Any claimed default under the Promissory Note;

    • Any demands made by Carl Fernstrom's line of credit lender;

    Carl Fernstrom shall not disturb, threaten, or interfere with Heather Fernstrom's possession and enjoyment of the Property. This Non-Disturbance Covenant is a material term of this Addendum and its breach shall entitle Heather Fernstrom to seek immediate injunctive relief without bond.
